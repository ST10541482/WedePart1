What We Do

Tshimologo Food Bank offers different community programmes that aim to reduce hunger and encourage people to help one another.

Food Collection

We collect non-perishable food donations from individuals, shops and local businesses.

Food Packing

Volunteers help sort and pack donated food into parcels for families who need assistance.

Community Food Drives

We organise food collection activities to encourage community participation.

Volunteer Programme

Community members can volunteer their time to assist with food collection and distribution.

Sponsorship

Businesses and individuals can support our organisation through food and financial sponsorship.

Food Awareness

We encourage people to understand food shortages and the importance of helping vulnerable families.

Become Part of Our Programmes

Your support can help us make a difference. You can participate by donating food, volunteering or becoming a sponsor.
