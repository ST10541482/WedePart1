What We Do

Tshimologo Food Bank provides different programmes that encourage people to work together to support families and individuals who need food assistance.

1. Food Collection

We collect non-perishable food such as rice, maize meal, pasta, canned vegetables, beans and other suitable food items.

2. Food Packing

Volunteers help sort and pack donated food into organised food parcels that can be distributed to community members.

3. Community Food Drives

Community food drives allow schools, businesses, churches and local groups to collect food together.

4. Volunteer Programme

Volunteers can assist with food collection, packing, organising donations and community activities.

5. Sponsorship

Businesses and individuals can support the organisation by providing financial assistance or useful resources.

6. Food Awareness

We encourage communities to learn about food support, sharing resources and helping people who are experiencing difficult circumstances.
