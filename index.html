Welcome to Tshimologo Food Bank

Tshimologo Food Bank is a community-focused organisation that helps provide food support to families and individuals facing difficult financial situations. The organisation works with volunteers, donors, local businesses and community members.

Our goal is to make sure that people in our community have access to basic food supplies while also encouraging people to support one another.

Our Main Programmes

Food Collection

We collect suitable food donations from individuals, businesses and community groups.After collection, the food is sorted and stored in our warehouse before being distributed to families and individuals in need.

Food Packing

Volunteers help organise donated food into food parcels for distribution.The food parcels are then distributed to families and individuals in need.When distributing food parcels, we ensure that the food is safe and suitable for consumption.
