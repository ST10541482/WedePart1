Welcome to Tshimologo Food Bank

Tshimologo Food Bank is a community-focused organisation that aims to reduce hunger and support families who experience food shortages. We believe that everyone deserves access to basic food supplies.

Through food donations, volunteers and community partnerships, we work towards creating a caring and supportive community.
