About Tshimologo Food Bank

Learn more about our organisation and what we do.

Our History

Tshimologo Food Bank is a fictional non-profit organisation created in 2024 as part of an academic website project. The organisation was created with the idea of bringing community members together to support families who may struggle to afford enough food.

The organisation focuses on collecting food donations, organising food parcels and connecting volunteers with community projects. Our Mission

Our mission is to collect and distribute food responsibly while supporting families and individuals in communities experiencing food shortages.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a caring community where people work together so that fewer families experience food insecurity.

Our Values

Compassion

We treat every person with kindness, dignity and understanding.

Teamwork

We believe that communities can achieve more when people work together.

Responsibility

We aim to use donated food and resources responsibly and carefully.

Respect

We respect all community members regardless of their background or circumstances.

Who We Support

Our target audience includes families, individuals, elderly community members and other people who may need temporary food assistance.

We also work with volunteers, businesses, schools, community groups and individuals who would like to contribute to food support projects.
