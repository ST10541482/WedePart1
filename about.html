About Us

Learn more about our organisation and our purpose.We are a community-based non-profit organisation dedicated to supporting families and individuals experiencing food shortages. The organisation was founded in 2010 by a group of local residents who wanted to make a positive impact in their community. Since then, we have grown and expanded our programmes to reach more families in need. Our organisation focuses on collecting food donations and distributing food parcels to people who need assistance. We also encourage community involvement and volunteering to help reduce hunger in our area.Every donation and volunteer effort contributes to our mission of building stronger communities and providing essential support to those in need.The organisation is committed to transparency and accountability, ensuring that all donations are used effectively to support those in need. We work closely with local businesses, schools, and community groups to raise awareness about food insecurity and to encourage participation in our initiatives. When you support Tshimologo Food Bank, you are helping to make a difference in the lives of families and individuals who are struggling to access basic food supplies.

Who We Are

Tshimologo Food Bank is a community-focused organisation made up of people who share the same goal of helping individuals and families experiencing food insecurity. We work with volunteers, donors, local businesses, community leaders and other organisations to provide food assistance to people who need it most. Our work involves collecting donated food, checking and organising the items, preparing food parcels and distributing them to deserving households. We also organise food donation campaigns and community activities to encourage more people to participate in fighting hunger. We understand that food assistance is not only about giving people something to eat, but also about showing care, respect and support during challenging times. We aim to treat everyone fairly and protect the dignity of the people who receive assistance. Our organisation values teamwork because we know that we cannot fight hunger alone. Every volunteer, donor and supporter plays an important role in helping us reach more families. Through our daily efforts, we hope to build a stronger community where people look after one another and work together to improve the lives of those around them.

At Tshimologo Food Bank, we believe that food is a basic human need and that every person deserves the opportunity to have regular meals and live a healthy life. We believe that nobody should be judged or treated differently because they are experiencing poverty or financial difficulties. Hunger can affect people from different backgrounds, ages and circumstances, and anyone may need assistance at some point in their lives. For this reason, we believe in treating every person fairly and providing support with kindness and respect. Our organisation aims to create an environment where individuals and families can receive assistance without feeling embarrassed or excluded. Compassion means understanding the difficulties that other people may be experiencing and being willing to help them. We believe that every person deserves to be treated with care, patience and understanding. We also believe in kindness because even small actions can have a positive effect on someone's life. Whether it is donating a food item, helping to prepare food parcels or giving a few hours of volunteer work, every contribution matters. We want to encourage people to look beyond their own needs and recognise that there are others in the community who may require support. Through compassion and kindness, we hope to inspire more people to become involved in helping others. We also strongly believe in honesty, trust and responsibility. People who donate food or support our organisation need to feel confident that their contributions are being used for the correct purpose. We therefore believe in handling donations carefully and making responsible decisions when distributing food assistance. We aim to be transparent about our activities and maintain good relationships with our donors, volunteers and community partners. Responsibility also means making sure that donated food is stored and handled properly and that suitable food items are distributed to the people who need them. We understand that trust is important for the success of a non-profit organisation, and we want to maintain that trust through our actions.Another important belief is equality and respect. We believe that every person has value and deserves to be treated with dignity, regardless of their financial situation, age, gender, background or personal circumstances. Our organisation aims to support people fairly and without discrimination. We also believe that everyone should have an opportunity to contribute to the community in their own way. Some people may be able to donate food or money, while others may be able to offer their time, skills or knowledge. We value all forms of support because each contribution helps us move closer to our goal of reducing hunger.Lastly, we believe in teamwork and community involvement. Fighting hunger is not something that one person or organisation can achieve alone. It requires cooperation between individuals, families, businesses, volunteers, schools, community groups and other organisations. When people work together, they can collect more donations, reach more households and create stronger support networks. We believe that a community becomes stronger when its members care for one another and are willing to offer help during difficult times. Through our beliefs and values, Tshimologo Food Bank aims to make a positive difference in people's lives and contribute towards a community where everyone has access to food and the opportunity to live with dignity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to reduce hunger and food insecurity by collecting, organising and distributing food donations to vulnerable individuals and families in our communities. We aim to provide assistance to households that experience financial difficulties and struggle to afford enough food for their daily needs. We are committed to working with volunteers, donors, local businesses and community organisations to make food assistance available to those who need it. We also aim to raise awareness about hunger and encourage people to become part of the solution by giving their time, resources and support. Through responsible food distribution, community involvement and strong partnerships, we want to improve the well-being of families and provide hope to people facing difficult circumstances.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a caring and supportive community where no individual or family has to go hungry because they cannot afford food. We hope to grow Tshimologo Food Bank into a trusted organisation that can reach more households and provide reliable assistance to people experiencing food shortages. We want to develop strong partnerships with local businesses, supermarkets, community groups and other organisations so that we can collect more donations and expand our programmes. In the future, we hope to introduce more food donation campaigns, volunteer opportunities and community support activities. We also want to encourage a culture where people understand the importance of sharing and helping others. Our long-term dream is to contribute towards a healthier, stronger and more united community where everyone has the opportunity to access food and live with dignity.
