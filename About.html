About Us


A food bank is a place where food is normally collected from different types of sources like shops,farmers or individuals and they are then shared among people who are less fortune and don’t have enough money to buy food to eat.The business is a non-profit ,basically meaning that the food is not sold to the people they are given to,it is given to the families ,schools and community groups for free.The important main aim of the food bank is to fight hunger and make sure that no one in the community ever goes with no food at all.


The food banks main job is to gather, store/keep and then distribute food. They receive donations of items like rice, bread, tinned food, and sometimes fruit from supermarkets, people who produce food and people in the community. The volunteers then start sorting and packing the food so it can be handed out equally to the families that need it the most. Some food banks also prepare meals and deliver them to schools or shelters for the homeless people. They usually organize food drives and campaigns to encourage people to donate what they can. One of the biggest challenge they face is that not everyone knows how to reach them and help, so this is where a website comes in and would play a very important role. The website could show donation drop off points, give updates of which items are mostly needed and also allow people to sign up as volunteers. The families in need could also use the website to find out when the next food distribution will happen, in this manner the website would make the food bank more visible, more organized as it reduces a lot of confusion with no website, and most important in helping people.


One of the best thing about our food bank is that it is run by volunteers, people who give up their time to help others. Volunteers do all sorts of jobs like collecting food, sorting it, packing it and handing it out. They also help with fundraising and raising awareness about the food bank in the community. The volunteers are very important because without them the food bank would not be able to operate and help those in need.   • Our Mission: To provide food assistance to those in need within our community.
  • Our Vision: A community where everyone has access to nutritious food.
  • Our Values: Compassion, Respect, Integrity, and Collaboration.
  • Our History: Founded in 2005, our food bank has been serving the community for over 15 years.
  • Our Impact: We have distributed millions of pounds of food to those in need.
  • Our Future: We are committed to expanding our services and reaching more families in need.
  • Our Commitment: We are dedicated to fighting hunger and improving food security in our community.
    • We will continue to advocate for policies that support food access and equity.
    • We will strengthen our partnerships with local organizations to enhance our impact.
    • We will engage the community in our mission and encourage volunteerism.
