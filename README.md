The food banks main job is to gather, store/keep and then distribute food. They receive donations of items like rice, bread, tinned food, and sometimes fruit from supermarkets, people who produce food and people in the community. The volunteers then start sorting and packing the food so it can be handed out equally to the families that need it the most. Some food banks also prepare meals and deliver them to schools or shelters for the homeless people. They usually organize food drives and campaigns to encourage people to donate what they can. One of the biggest challenge they face is that not everyone knows how to reach them and help, so this is where a website comes in and would play a very important role. Timeline

In the first week, the main  focus will be on planning what the website should look like and gathering everything needed. This means thinking about the purpose of the information, encourage donations, or I would also collect details about the NPO, such as their background, mission, pictures, and contact information. At the same time, I would look at other NPO websites for ideas and inspiration. By the end of this week, I would have a clear plan and all the content ready to use.

Week 2 – Designing and Building the Website
The second week would be about actually creating the website. First, I would sketch or plan out how each page should look, choosing colors, fonts, and layouts that fit the NPO’s identity. Then, I would start building the pages, such as Home, About Us, Programs, and Contact. Important features like a donations section or a volunteer sign-up form would also be added. I would make sure the website works properly on both computers and mobile phones, since many people access the internet with their phones.

Week 3 – Testing and Launching
In the final week, I would check the whole website to make sure everything works well. This means testing links, fixing spelling mistakes, and checking that forms are working. Once the website is ready, it would be launched for the public to use. I would also help the NPO staff or volunteers learn how to update the site, and then share the website link on social media so more people know about it. After launching, the website can be updated regularly with new events, donation drives, or success stories.
